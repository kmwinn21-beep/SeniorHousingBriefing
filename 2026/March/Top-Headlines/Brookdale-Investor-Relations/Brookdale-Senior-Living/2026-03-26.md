# Brookdale Reports Occupancy Up 280bps YoY to 82.1%; Completes $600M Refinancing

**Date:** 2026-03-26
**Source:** Brookdale Investor Relations / McKnight's Senior Living
**URL:** https://brookdaleinvestors.com/news/news-details/2026/Brookdale-Reports-February-2026-Occupancy/default.aspx

## Summary
Brookdale Senior Living reported February 2026 occupancy rose 280 basis points year-over-year to 82.1%, outperforming typical seasonal trends, as the operator's downsizing strategy continues to show results. The company also completed a $600 million refinancing that eliminated all 2026 debt maturities, while simultaneously acquiring 41 previously-leased communities for $610 million. Brookdale remains the largest US senior living operator as it executes a focused 3-pronged strategy targeting owned communities in 2026.
