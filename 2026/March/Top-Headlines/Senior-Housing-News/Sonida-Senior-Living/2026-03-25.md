# Sonida Senior Living Completes $1.8 Billion Merger with CNL Healthcare Properties

**Date:** 2026-03-25
**Source:** Senior Housing News
**URL:** https://investors.sonidaseniorliving.com/news/news-details/2026/Sonida-Senior-Living-Completes-1-8-Billion-Strategic-Merger-with-CNL-Healthcare-Properties-Inc-/default.aspx

## Summary
Sonida Senior Living officially closed its $1.8 billion merger with CNL Healthcare Properties on March 11, 2026, creating the eighth-largest U.S. senior housing owner-operator. The combined portfolio includes 153 communities (~14,700 owned units) spanning independent living, assisted living, and memory care, with a combined enterprise value of approximately $3 billion. The deal establishes Sonida as a major pure-play senior housing platform with significantly expanded geographic reach and capital access.
