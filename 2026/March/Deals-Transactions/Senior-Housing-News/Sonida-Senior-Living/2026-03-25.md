# Sonida Senior Living Closes $1.8B Merger with CNL Healthcare Properties — Creates 8th-Largest U.S. Owner-Operator

**Date:** 2026-03-25
**Source:** Senior Housing News
**URL:** https://investors.sonidaseniorliving.com/news/news-details/2026/Sonida-Senior-Living-Completes-1-8-Billion-Strategic-Merger-with-CNL-Healthcare-Properties-Inc-/default.aspx

## Summary
Sonida Senior Living completed its merger with CNL Healthcare Properties on March 11, 2026, in a cash-and-stock transaction valued at approximately $1.8 billion. The combined platform now operates 153 IL/AL/MC communities with ~14,700 owned units, creating a $3 billion enterprise and the eighth-largest U.S. senior housing owner-operator. The deal establishes a pure-play senior housing platform with expanded scale, geographic reach, and capital access.
