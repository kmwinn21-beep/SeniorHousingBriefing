# Brookdale Completes $600M Refinancing, Acquires 41 Communities for $610M

**Date:** 2026-03-26
**Source:** McKnight's Senior Living / Ehpad Magazine
**URL:** https://www.mcknightsseniorliving.com/news/brookdale-senior-living-continues-downsizing-with-focus-on-owning-communities/

## Summary
America's largest senior living operator, Brookdale Senior Living, executed a major $600 million refinancing that eliminated all 2026 debt maturities, simultaneously acquiring 41 previously-leased communities from its portfolio for $610 million. The transaction is part of Brookdale's ongoing strategic shift toward owning rather than leasing communities, reducing operating risk and expanding its owned asset base. The 41-community acquisition adds significantly to Brookdale's owned portfolio as occupancy climbs toward stabilization targets.
