# Marketers Grapple with Outdated Perceptions of Senior Living as 'Nursing Homes and Cruise Ships'

**Date:** 2026-03-25
**Source:** Senior Housing News
**URL:** https://seniorhousingnews.com/2026/03/20/marketers-grapple-with-outdated-perception-of-senior-living-as-nursing-homes-cruise-ships/

## Summary
Senior living marketers are struggling to overcome persistent public misconceptions that conflate modern senior living with institutional nursing homes or over-programmed cruise ships. Leaders are pushing for more authentic, lifestyle-focused marketing strategies that resonate with incoming boomer residents. The shift represents a broader strategic challenge: changing the public narrative before a new wave of supply hits the market.
