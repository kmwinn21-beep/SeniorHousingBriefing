# Sonida Senior Living Closes $1.8B Merger with CNL Healthcare, Creating $3B Owner-Operator

**Date:** 2026-03-26
**Source:** Senior Housing News / BusinessWire
**URL:** https://seniorhousingnews.com/2026/03/11/quite-the-journey-sonida-closes-1-8m-cnl-deal-pushes-labor-optimization-forward/

## Summary
Sonida Senior Living completed its $1.8 billion merger with CNL Healthcare Properties, creating one of the largest senior housing companies in the US with an estimated $3 billion total enterprise value. The combined company owns, manages, or is invested in 165 communities with over 16,400 units across 35 states, with a strengthened presence in the South, Southeast, Midwest, Mountain West, Pacific Northwest, and Mid-Atlantic. The deal establishes Sonida as a true national-scale pure-play senior housing owner-operator with significant growth runway.
