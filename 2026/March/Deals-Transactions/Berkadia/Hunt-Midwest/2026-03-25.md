# Berkadia Facilitates Sale of Six-Property Assisted Living Portfolio in Kansas City MSA

**Date:** 2026-03-25
**Source:** Berkadia Seniors Housing & Healthcare
**URL:** https://www.berkadia.com/news/senior-living-portfolio-kansas-city-msa-mo-sold-by-berkadia-2026/

## Summary
Berkadia Seniors Housing & Healthcare brokered the March 23, 2026 sale of six assisted living and memory care communities totaling 413 units in the Kansas City MSA on behalf of a joint venture between Hunt Midwest and Principal Senior Living. The purpose-built communities (constructed 2015–2019) are located in affluent suburban Kansas City markets in Missouri and Kansas. The buyer was not publicly disclosed.
