# Assisted Living Wage Growth Slows as Turnover Rates Continue to Improve

**Date:** 2026-03-26
**Source:** Senior Housing News
**URL:** https://seniorhousingnews.com/2026/01/30/assisted-living-wage-growth-slows-turnover-improving/

## Summary
Wage growth for assisted living nursing positions decelerated in 2025, with medication aides seeing a 3.15% hourly pay increase compared to 3.97% in 2024, signaling a potential stabilization after years of wage inflation. At the same time, turnover rates across the sector continue on a downward trajectory, driven by culture improvements, benefits investments, and a more stable labor market. Labor costs still represent approximately 55% of total operating expenses for senior living operators, making ongoing efficiency and retention strategies critical.
