# 2026 Could See Record M&A Activity in Senior Living and Care, Experts Say

**Date:** 2026-03-26
**Source:** McKnight's Senior Living
**URL:** https://www.mcknightsseniorliving.com/news/2026-could-see-record-acquisitions-in-senior-living-and-care-experts-say/

## Summary
Industry experts are projecting that 2026 could surpass 2025's record deal-making activity in senior living and care, with new investor capital flooding into the sector. Senior housing occupancy near 90% nationally, combined with thin construction pipelines, is driving aggressive acquisition interest from private equity, REITs, and owner-operators. Deal pricing is trending upward with rising cap rate compression as buyers compete for stabilized assets.
