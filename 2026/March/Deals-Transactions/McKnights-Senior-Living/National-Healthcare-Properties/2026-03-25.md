# National Healthcare Properties Announces $64M SHOP Acquisition with Discovery Senior Living

**Date:** 2026-03-25
**Source:** McKnight's Senior Living
**URL:** https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/03/03/3248299/0/en/National-Healthcare-Properties-Announces-64-million-SHOP-Acquisition.html

## Summary
National Healthcare Properties (NHP) announced a $64 million acquisition of a 13-community, 592-unit assisted living portfolio across eight states, structured as a RIDEA joint venture with Discovery Senior Living as the continuing operator. NHP will hold ~98.5% of the JV and retains a right of first refusal on 13 additional communities (~500 units). The transaction is expected to close in Q2 2026 and will represent approximately 40% of NHP's total SHOP cash NOI.
