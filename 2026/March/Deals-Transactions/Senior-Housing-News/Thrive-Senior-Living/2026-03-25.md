# Thrive Senior Living Acquires Three Georgia Communities, Expanding Home-State Portfolio to 8

**Date:** 2026-03-25
**Source:** Senior Housing News / Seniors Housing Business
**URL:** https://seniorshousingbusiness.com/thrive-senior-living-acquires-three-communities-in-georgia/

## Summary
Thrive Senior Living acquired three newly built AL communities in Cochran, Thomson, and Jesup, Georgia — operating under the "At Home Senior Living" brand — from the Sawyer family. The communities were 94% occupied at acquisition, were developed between 2019 and 2022, and were financed by SRP Federal Credit Union. The deal expands Thrive's Georgia footprint to eight communities, continuing their position as one of the more active regional operators in the Southeast.
