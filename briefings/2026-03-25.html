AHCA / NCAL
2025–2026 Nursing Home Report Reveals Lower Turnover Across Key Departments
AHCA/NCAL's 2025-2026 Nursing Home Salary & Benefits Report shows turnover declining across almost all departments. Top-level executive turnover fell from 32% to 22%, CNA turnover eased from 44% to 42%, and sign-on bonus usage dropped from 65% to 56% of facilities. The report covers 917 nursing homes and 111,600+ employees, suggesting the labor market is gradually stabilizing.

Health Affairs / LDI Penn
Study: Staffing Mandates Boost Care Quality Without Triggering Nursing Home Closures
A landmark Health Affairs study (March 3, 2026) examined nearly 6,900 nursing homes across 22 states over 14 years and found that minimum staffing mandates consistently raised care levels without causing closures. The findings challenge the argument that staffing requirements force closures, and will likely reignite federal and state legislative discussions around minimum staffing thresholds.
