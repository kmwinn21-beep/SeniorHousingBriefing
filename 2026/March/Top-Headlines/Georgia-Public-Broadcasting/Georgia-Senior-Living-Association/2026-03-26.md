# Georgia Becomes One of First States to Protect Senior Living Consumers

**Date:** 2026-03-26
**Source:** Georgia Public Broadcasting
**URL:** https://www.gpb.org/news/2026/03/25/georgia-one-of-the-first-states-law-protecting-people-seeking-senior-living

## Summary
Georgia Governor Brian Kemp signed Senate Bill 439 into law, making Georgia one of the first states to require senior living referral agencies to disclose their business model, financial relationships, and that families may have options beyond those they present. The law targets referral agencies that charge 75–115%+ of one month's rent as placement fees without clearly disclosing this to families. The legislation is seen as a model for other states and was championed by the Georgia Senior Living Association.
