## Summary
Following its $1.8 billion merger with CNL Healthcare Properties, Sonida Senior Living is prioritizing a labor optimization model that rewards top-performing employees and reduces costly turnover. The company reported that investments in higher wages, improved benefits, and a supportive culture have stabilized leadership turnover, with Q4 2025 trends carrying favorably into 2026. Sonida's labor strategy is a key area of focus as it integrates 165 communities across 35 states into a unified operational model.
