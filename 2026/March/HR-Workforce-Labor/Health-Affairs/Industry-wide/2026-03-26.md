# New Study: State Staffing Mandates Raised Care Levels Without Triggering Nursing Home Closures

**Date:** 2026-03-26
**Source:** Health Affairs / LDI Penn
**URL:** https://ldi.upenn.edu/our-work/research-updates/staffing-mandates-boost-care-without-closing-nursing-homes/

## Summary
A landmark study published in Health Affairs examined nearly 6,900 nursing homes across 22 states over 14 years, finding that state minimum staffing mandates raised care quality without causing facility closures. The research challenges the long-held industry argument that staffing mandates are economically unviable, and comes as the federal staffing mandate rollback remains a topic of debate. For operators, the findings reinforce the need for proactive workforce planning and compliance readiness as state-level mandates continue to evolve.
