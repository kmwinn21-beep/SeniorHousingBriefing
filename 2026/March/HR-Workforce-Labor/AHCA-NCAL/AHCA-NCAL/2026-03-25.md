# 2025–2026 Nursing Home Report Reveals Lower Turnover Across Key Departments

**Date:** 2026-03-25
**Source:** AHCA/NCAL
**URL:** https://www.ahcancal.org/News-and-Communications/Blog/Pages/2025%E2%80%932026-Nursing-Home-Report-Reveals-Lower-Turnover.aspx

## Summary
AHCA/NCAL's 2025-2026 Nursing Home Salary & Benefits Report shows turnover declining across almost all departments. Top-level executive turnover fell from 32% to 22%, CNA turnover eased from 44% to 42%, and the share of facilities offering sign-on bonuses dropped from 65% to 56%. The report covers 917 nursing homes and 111,600+ employees, suggesting the labor market is gradually stabilizing even as staffing challenges persist.
