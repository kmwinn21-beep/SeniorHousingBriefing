# PACS Group CEO Says Company Emerged Stronger from Federal Billing Investigation

**Date:** 2026-03-26
**Source:** Skilled Nursing News
**URL:** https://skillednursingnews.com/2026/03/time-of-crisis-nursing-home-giant-pacs-ceo-on-emerging-stronger-from-a-federal-investigation/

## Summary
PACS Group CEO Jason Murray publicly addressed the nation's largest nursing home operator's recovery from its federal billing investigation, stating the company removed $65 million in revenue and installed new compliance guardrails. Despite the crisis, PACS grew to 323 facilities across 17 states and posted $5.29 billion in 2025 revenue — a 29% year-over-year jump. Murray characterized the federal investigation as "still active but not introducing new risks" and said the internal audit is now complete.
