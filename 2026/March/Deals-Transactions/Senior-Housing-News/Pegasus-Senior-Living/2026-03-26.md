# Pegasus Senior Living Surpasses 50-Community Mark with 10 New Brookdale-Managed Properties

**Date:** 2026-03-26
**Source:** Senior Housing News
**URL:** https://seniorhousingnews.com/2026/03/03/pegasus-senior-living-adds-10-communities-surpassing-50-community-mark/

## Summary
Pegasus Senior Living crossed the 50-community threshold after signing an agreement to manage 10 former Brookdale Senior Living properties, marking a significant growth milestone for the Dallas-based operator. The expansion reflects the broader trend of portfolio reshuffling as larger operators like Brookdale divest or transfer management of non-core communities to regional operators. Pegasus is now one of the faster-growing mid-size operators in the sector.
