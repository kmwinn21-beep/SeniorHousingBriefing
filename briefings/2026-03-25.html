Welltower Closes $1.2B Assisted Living Portfolio Acquisition
Welltower (NYSE: WELL) announced the completion of a $1.2 billion acquisition of a 28-property assisted living portfolio spanning six states. The deal, first disclosed in January, was financed through a combination of senior secured debt and equity. CEO Shankh Mitra said the properties average 92% occupancy and are concentrated in high-barrier Sun Belt markets. The transaction adds approximately 3,400 units to Welltower's portfolio.

NIC Q1 2026 Data: National Occupancy Reaches Post-Pandemic High of 84.7%
NIC MAP Vision's Q1 2026 preliminary data shows national senior housing occupancy reached 84.7%, the highest level since Q3 2019. Assisted living led gains at 85.1%, while independent living occupancy stands at 87.2%. New construction starts declined 14% year-over-year as elevated construction costs and tighter lending standards continue to dampen development activity.

Ventas Divests 12-Property Portfolio to LCS for $890M
Ventas (NYSE: VTR) completed the sale of a 12-property continuing care retirement community (CCRC) portfolio to Life Care Services (LCS) for $890 million. The portfolio, located primarily in the Midwest and Southeast, totals 4,100 units. LCS will operate the communities under its existing Life Care Services brand. Ventas stated the proceeds will be used to pay down debt and fund targeted acquisitions in higher-growth markets.

Brookdale Reports 8% Reduction in Agency Staffing Costs in Q4 2025
Brookdale Senior Living (NYSE: BKD) reported that agency staffing costs fell 8% in Q4 2025 compared to the same period in 2024, driven by direct-hire campaigns and improved retention programs. Despite the improvement, agency usage still represents approximately 11% of total labor spend. CEO Cindy Baier noted that markets in the Pacific Northwest and Northeast remain the most challenging for direct hiring. The company expanded its internal travel nurse program to 38 additional communities.

LeadingAge Urges Congress to Pass Nursing Home Staffing Relief Legislation
LeadingAge joined a coalition of 14 senior care associations urging Congress to pass relief legislation addressing CMS's 2024 nursing home minimum staffing mandate. The coalition argues the mandate is "operationally unworkable" without additional funding for workforce pipeline programs. A Senate committee hearing is scheduled for April 8. Separately, LeadingAge released a workforce survey showing 71% of members report "moderate to severe" difficulty filling CNAs and care aides.

Atria Senior Living Breaks Ground on $145M Mixed-Use Community in Austin
Atria Senior Living broke ground on a $145 million, 310-unit independent and assisted living community in Austin, Texas, its first ground-up development in the market. The 14-story tower will include approximately 8,000 sq ft of ground-floor retail and is expected to open in Q3 2028. Atria partnered with The Related Companies on the project. The company cited Austin's demographic tailwinds — a 65+ population growing at 5.2% annually — as a key driver of the investment.
