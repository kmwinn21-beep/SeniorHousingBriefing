# New Study: Staffing Mandates Boost Care Quality Without Triggering Nursing Home Closures

**Date:** 2026-03-25
**Source:** Health Affairs / LDI Penn
**URL:** https://ldi.upenn.edu/our-work/research-updates/staffing-mandates-boost-care-without-closing-nursing-homes/

## Summary
A landmark study published in the March 3, 2026 issue of Health Affairs examined nearly 6,900 nursing homes across 22 states over 14 years and found that minimum staffing mandates consistently raised care levels without causing facility closures. The findings directly challenge the long-standing industry argument that staffing requirements would force closures. This research will likely reignite federal and state legislative discussions around minimum staffing thresholds for skilled nursing and assisted living providers.
