# CMS Nursing Home Staffing Campaign More Than Doubles to $200M

**Date:** 2026-03-25
**Source:** McKnight's Long-Term Care News
**URL:** https://www.mcknights.com/news/breaking-cms-nursing-home-staffing-campaign-more-than-doubles-to-200m/

## Summary
CMS Administrator Mehmet Oz announced the agency's nursing home staffing campaign budget has surged from $75M to $200M, with applications due March 27, 2026 and program launch expected July 1, 2026. The expanded campaign offers RNs and LPN/LVNs up to $40,000 in tuition reimbursement for a 3-year commitment to qualifying nursing homes. All 50 governors were engaged to contribute matching funds, with particular focus on rural underserved areas.
